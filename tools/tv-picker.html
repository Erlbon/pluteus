pluteus tv picker

Browse the public channel catalogue of iptv-org, try a channel, tick the ones you want, and save them as .m3u files into a library folder (one file per channel), or as one list for Kodi. pluteus plays an HLS channel in its video page. This page runs only in your browser: it reads the catalogue from iptv-org.github.io and the channels you try or test from their own servers. Channels are listed by the catalogue as publicly available; they stay the property of their broadcasters.
