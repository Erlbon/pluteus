Find TV channels

Browse the public channel catalogue of iptv-org, try a channel, tick the ones you want, and save them into your upload folder as .m3u files (one per channel, in IPTV). pluteus plays an HLS channel in its video page. Your browser reads the catalogue from iptv-org.github.io and the channels you try or test from their own servers; pluteus only writes the files. Channels are listed by the catalogue as publicly available; they stay the property of their broadcasters.

    Logos for stations you already saved

    Looks through your IPTV folder for station files without a logo beside them, finds the logo and saves it next to the file. A file that already has a picture is left alone.
